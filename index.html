Selected Academic Work

  • Master's Thesis

    Investigated aromatic, antiaromatic, and non-aromatic π-systems using ωB97X-D/cc-pVQZ calculations with Gaussian, ORCA, and GIMIC.

  • Solvent Effects

    Evaluated benzene aromaticity across 13 solvents using the SMD implicit solvation model and observed systematic enhancement of diatropic current with increasing solvent polarity.

  • Exploratory Study

    Worked on vibronic coupling Hamiltonian modeling for excited-state acetylene photochemistry, including exposure to Jahn-Teller and pseudo-Jahn-Teller interactions.
